Base ship. Heating peaks at 16103 m and 798 m/s — not at max dynamic pressure, which is five times lower at 2875 m. Heating goes as √density × speed³; dynamic pressure as density × speed².

Why the commit floor is the lever

Armstrong's Satellite on a single pass, against how deep the player commits. Peak heat falls steeply as the entry gets shallower — that is the escape route the floor closes. Without it the player enters at about 19,700 m, the plunge reads about 128, and no capacity can tell it apart from a skimmed descent.

The floor sits at 8000 m — left of this curve's cheap end. Everything to the right of it is what the rule takes away.
